Exodus is an open-world PC game that lets us talk with the Israelites before the crossing of the Red Sea. The mission is to find Moses' staff by drawing out the undocumented stories of the subaltern people around us. Made with Unity.

## The game

@embed https://www-ccv.adobe.io/v1/player/ccv/4sfjw43Stj9/embed?bgcolor=%23191919&lazyLoading=true&api_key=BehancePro2View | Gameplay: moving through the camp and talking to the people in it.

Every character has their own account of what happened, and none of them agrees with the version in the book. The staff is found by listening rather than by searching — the NPCs are driven by AI, so a conversation can go somewhere the script did not plan for.
