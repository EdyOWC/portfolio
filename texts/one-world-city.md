What if we could solve the global environmental crisis by radically changing our lifestyle?

One World City is my final project for the M.F.A. at Tel Aviv University.

OWC is a trans-platform experience demonstrating a single situation: the global population moves to one city, reducing the footprint of mankind to a limited, ultra-comfortable space.

## Five platforms, one world

1. A commercial for the real estate project
2. A TV sitcom
3. A tablet app for writing songs with generative AI
4. A VR experience
5. All of the above, inside an exhibition space

Are you ready to see what the future holds?
